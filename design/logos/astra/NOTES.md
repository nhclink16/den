# Den logo concepts

All five are transparent, standalone 64 × 64 SVGs with no fonts or external assets. They use Den's amber `#e8a44a` throughout; the supporting shape switches from charcoal `#1b1916` in light mode to cream `#ece5d8` in dark mode through `prefers-color-scheme`.

- **01-doorway-d.svg — Doorway d.** A broad lowercase d with an open, amber doorway cut through its bowl. The sturdy letterform complements Zilla Slab without depending on a font; this is my pick for the main identity.
- **02-lamp-on.svg — Lamp on.** A table lamp with its amber shade lit and a wide, stable base. It makes the app's “dim room, live things lit” visual language literal.
- **03-fox-den.svg — Fox den.** Two pointed ears and a broad cheek shape suggest a fox without eyes, whiskers, or other details that disappear in a browser tab. This is the mascot direction.
- **04-close-circle.svg — Close circle.** Five broad segments gather around one shared space, with one segment lit amber. An abstract mark for a small friend group and its persistent room.
- **05-couch-party.svg — Couch party.** A chunky armchair with one amber cushion evokes a seat saved for you in the hangout. It puts the shared living-room feeling ahead of gaming hardware.
