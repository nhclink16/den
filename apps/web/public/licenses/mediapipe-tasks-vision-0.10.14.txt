MediaPipe Tasks Vision 0.10.14 and the Selfie Segmenter model

Background blur is MediaPipe's selfie segmenter, reached through
@livekit/track-processors. Den serves its own copies of both parts instead of
fetching them from Google's CDN at runtime:

  /blur/wasm/                  @mediapipe/tasks-vision 0.10.14 WebAssembly,
                               staged from node_modules by scripts/blur-assets.mjs
  /blur/selfie_segmenter.tflite  Selfie Segmenter (float16), downloaded once from
                               https://storage.googleapis.com/mediapipe-models/
                               image_segmenter/selfie_segmenter/float16/latest/
